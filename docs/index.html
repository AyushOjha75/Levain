Levain

Keep a starter alive. Bake better bread.

An Android app for sourdough and focaccia. Your cultures, their history, and the maths — all on your phone, none of it on anyone's server.

Download the latest APK Source on GitHub

Android 8.0+. Sideloaded — not on the Play Store. No account, no network permission, no telemetry.

What it does today

It remembers, so you don't have to.

Starters with faces

Every culture gets a jar avatar whose mood follows its real health — sleepy when sluggish, hungry when overdue.

Two-tap feeding

Pre-filled from last time, with chips for back-filling the feed you forgot to log this morning.

Honest streaks

Each feeding records the interval in force when it happened, so moving a jar to the fridge never rewrites its history.

Reminders that don't nag

One coalesced notification when starters come due. Fired once — the dashboard is the standing signal.

Baker's maths

Levain build and baker's percentage calculators, plus a home-screen widget showing who needs feeding.

Your data, exportable

One zip with everything including photos. Import restores it atomically — a failed restore leaves what you had.

Where it's going

Toward a guided bake.

  1. ShippedStarters, feedings, observations, reminders, insights and backup. The maintenance loop, done properly.
  2. ShippedIdentity and design system. A real launcher icon, and one palette, type scale and component set across the app.
  3. ShippedThe bake-centric data model. Recipes, snapshotted steps, and bakes that can be in progress — migrated without losing a row.
  4. NextRecipes as content. Sourdough and focaccia as hand-readable data, so adding a bread is never a code change.
  5. ThenThe guided bake. Levain build to oven, with timers for what a clock can measure and cues for what only you can judge.

Built in the open at github.com/AyushOjha75/Levain. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Room; no backend anywhere.
